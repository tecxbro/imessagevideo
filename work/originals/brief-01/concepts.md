# Brief-01 concepts — two genuinely different structures

## Concept A — "The Negotiation Reveal" (chosen)

**Premise:** The film follows ONE sale — a bike — but its centerpiece is a
*cutaway*: at the moment offers stall, we leave the user's thread and watch,
for ~8 seconds, the agent actually negotiating with the buyer in a second
conversation we were never shown. Then we come back for the payoff.

**Opening:** Cold, inside the user's normal thread — they send a bike photo +
"sell this for me?" A listing card grows from the message. The product promise
lands here (~5s) through the object, not a tagline.

**Structure:**
1. User thread: photo → listing card ($140) → "Listed"
2. Lowball offers trickle in as offer chips ($40 Nina, $60 Marcus); agent:
   "I can work with this." — personality beat, no user input needed
3. **CUTAWAY (act break):** iOS push-navigation into the agent↔buyer thread.
   We watch it work: Nina $80 → agent counters with reasoning ("frame alone is
   worth more") → Nina $100 tonight → agent "110, cash, tonight. In or out?" →
   Nina: "fine. 110." — tight, 4 exchanges
4. Back in user thread: "$110 — take it?" card with [Take it]/[Keep haggling]
   buttons. USER taps (the one decision the film gives them) → chip becomes an
   outgoing message
5. Payoff cascade: SOLD stamp slams onto the listing card → shipping-label
   card → "$110 sent" cash beat
6. Endcard: "Text it a photo. It handles the haggling." + offload.chat

**Why it's strong:** the cutaway is an editorial move none of the six
references use — it makes the invisible work *visible* for exactly long enough
to be delightful, then leaves. The user's single tap reframes agency: the film
says "you decide, it does." Object carries: photo→listing→offers→deal
card→label. The SOLD stamp is the one loud accent.

## Concept B — "The Cleanout" (parked)

**Premise:** Speedrun montage — three items sell in escalating rhythm:
bike (normal), lamp (fast), a ceramic frog (absurd, sells for the most).
"SOLD" stamps accumulate like a scoreboard; ends on the frog's $180 price
reveal.

**Opening:** identical photo-send mechanism, but the film's energy is
repetition-as-chorus (like 03's refrain) with stamp impacts as percussion.

**Why parked:** it's structurally a sibling of 05-linq's vignette montage —
three mini-arcs cutting fast. Safe, fun, but it doesn't push the edit language
anywhere the library hasn't been. Concept A's two-thread cutaway is the more
original device and gives the blind screening something real to react to.
If A confuses viewers, B is the fallback shape.
