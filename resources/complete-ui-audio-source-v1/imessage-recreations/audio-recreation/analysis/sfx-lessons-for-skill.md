# Sound-recreation lessons to add to the project skill

- Rebuild sound from measured timing and a newly created palette. Keep reference analysis separate from new audio assets; never silently mux the original reference soundtrack and label it recreated.
- Probe real audio start/duration and sample rate; output picture duration governs the new master. Do not copy a longer AAC tail or overlook deliberate quiet logo holds.
- Spectral onsets are not automatically sound effects. A music beat often coincides with motion cuts. Compare recurrence, frequency balance and content-aware analysis; keep music/percussion/ambiguous effects separate.
- Distinguish measured acoustic attack time/strength from proposed effect category, envelope length and mix gain. If listening is unavailable, say so and preserve low source-presence confidence.
- Write output-time cue sheets only after applying the exact picture edit map. For Rene, remove source34.9666667–41s and subtract181/30 from later cue times.
- Diagnose detector failure before rerunning: a peak cap misses weak masked effects, tempo has half/double/subdivision ambiguity, and overlapping music corrupts simple tail-duration estimates. Record these limits and avoid claiming exhaustive effects coverage.
- Start the new mix with sparse, quiet sends/selections/large transitions. Do not add a sound to every word, icon or bubble. A displayed audio waveform, cash panel or phone graphic does not prove audible speech, a cash register or ringing.
