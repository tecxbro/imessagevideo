# SFX analysis and palette

These sheets measure mixed reference audio and align optional, newly synthesized accents to visual context. No listening endpoint was available to this analysis worker. No isolated sound identity is asserted, and no reference waveform/sample is saved or reused. Root's content-aware audio captions may later refine attribution.

The detected recurring attacks are kept separate from the proposed cue list. Repetition is evidence for music/percussion, not proof that every off-grid event is UI sound. The180-peak cap favors strong attacks and can miss weak masked effects. Durations in the cue list are design lengths, not isolated source decay claims.

## Production constraints

Create all sounds from oscillators/noise/envelopes or newly licensed/generated material. Use short rounded message transients, dry taps, filtered air movement and restrained tonal confirmations. Avoid literal cash registers, phone ringing or speech inferred only from UI pictures. Keep UI accents quieter than main musical hits, with sparse short tails; do not cover every changing word or icon.
