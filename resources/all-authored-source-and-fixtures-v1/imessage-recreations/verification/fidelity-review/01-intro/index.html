## Findings and corrections

- The title detector admitted dark blue glyphs into the black Introducing mask and clipped centered early words with a fixed left ROI. Neutral RGB filtering and per-element ROIs recover the actual centered word, blue typing reveal and later push to the left.
- The whole-phrase outline lost the independent word entrances. With begins at80, just82, one83, click90; Your125, agent126, is128, everywhere130. Native word contours now carry independent measured tracks/lifetimes.
- The pill and cursor had false lifetimes from unrelated blue elements. The pill is45–119, cursor48–79. The cursor turns/scales; the pressed lighting is a separate measured pill state70–79. The first cursor48 is a clipped tip; its invisible body pose is inferred from49 and explicitly disclosed.
- Channel measurement used clipped viewport intersections as if they were physical artwork sizes. Canonical fully visible icons and separately measured native labels preserve physical scale, offscreen clipping and staggered exits. Reciprocal wrapper/child scales are keyed every native frame to avoid interpolation stretch.
- Phone exits at195; the free phrase begins with its first cyan fragment196 and separate black words197–198. The original free zoom from8s is retained.
- The CTA was appearing fully composed while source words enter separately, then the address rises as blurred gray letters and becomes cyan from left to right. The first native template-fit attempt was rejected: float32 summed-square cancellation produced impossible normalized correlations; float64 integrals and a correlation bound fixed that numerical error. A later image check still found real pale letters removed by the fit threshold. That version was also rejected.
- The accepted short URL transition uses328 independently bounded semantic letter-pose artworks at531–554; each node has its own source bounds, position, size and one-frame lifetime. There is no complete source-frame/video runtime asset.132 native semantic black-word contours preserve the exact On-us exit and CTA prefix poses at518–554. The settled original state resumes unchanged at555/18.5s.
