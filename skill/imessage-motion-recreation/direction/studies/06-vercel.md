# Run Your Backend — Vercel

Picture samples at 03.50, 04.40, 05.75, 06.65 and 07.55.

- **Before.** The scene has two labeled volumes that read as frontend and backend. A bright triangular face is already attached to the diagram.
- **Choice.** The labels disappear and the volumes visually shorten toward the solid face. The triangle stays trackable throughout and becomes the lone end mark by 07.55.
- **After.** The end mark reads as the surviving shape of the diagram the viewer was already considering. Recognition carries through the transition.
- **Intent guess.** The film tries to associate the service with the combined structure by keeping its mark physically inside the demonstration.
- **Alternative.** Replace the diagram with a logo on a separate end card. That gives the mark its own accent and removes the visual connection to the preceding action.
- **Change for Direction.** Test an ending that holds a real piece of the scene while the brand arrives. A held scene and an explicit mark can share the frame if they have clear reading priority.
