# Poke Apple Messages — Interaction

Picture samples at 13.50, 13.95, 15.30, 16.65 and 17.10.

- **Before.** The person chose Indian food. Poke asks whether it should find a spot and then asks, “where are you rn?” The active question is now a location.
- **Choice.** The frame closes in on the “Current Location” affordance around 15.30. The keyboard clears around 17.05. A shared-location card with a map pin is visible at 17.12 below the same question.
- **After.** The pin reads as the reply to the original question. The viewer can track what the person shared and how it relates to picking a restaurant.
- **Intent guess.** Keeping the unanswered prompt visible makes the new visual state readable without a title.
- **Alternative.** Cut directly to a standalone map. The audience might understand that finding a restaurant uses location, while losing who shared it or why it appeared then.
- **Change for Direction.** Use the first shot after a key answer to show what that answer leads someone to look for or do. For Keiki, the specific reply gives the next close-up its subject. Keep a visible cue that connects the two.
