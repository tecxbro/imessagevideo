## The seven-second interval

Frames210–419 are exactly210 frames /7 seconds: 29.2% of this24-second cut without a UI action. The locator progresses, but the information payoff (Arrived510) is deferred much longer than the earlier tap/send grammar prepares the viewer for. In the UI-only score this is a genuine negative-space decision: the previous send has decayed and there is nothing to sound until the second-phone typing. It gives the later interrupted message a pause, but also exposes the hold.

The alternative adds a deliberately quiet, nonmusical procedural air/motion texture only across210–419, with half-second edge fades and slow stereo drift. It supplies continuity under the moving location graphic without inventing keys, messages, real footsteps, or a proprietary Check In alert. Both scores retain the same21 UI accents. The texture cannot create a new visual beat or solve the underlying duration by itself. These are design intentions and frame-based editorial judgments, **not claims of human audio audition**.
