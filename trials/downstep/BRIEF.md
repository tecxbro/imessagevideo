# Downstep launch-film brief

Downstep is a fictional product for singers rehearsing with a band. In a one-to-one iMessage thread, a musician attaches a short reference melody and includes the text `down 2`. Downstep replies with a returned audio attachment containing the melody two semitones lower. The returned melody preserves the note onsets and durations. A musician can replay the two attachments and decide whether the lower phrase suits their voice.

This trial supports one local demonstration fixture. The supplied input is a synthetic four-note melody (MIDI 64, 67, 69, 67), with onsets at 0, 0.45, 0.9 and 1.35 seconds and respective note durations of 0.40, 0.40, 0.40 and 0.65 seconds. The same 2.4-second attachment played back after a valid `down 2` has MIDI pitches 62, 65, 67 and 65, matching those timings. Exercise this exact path in code and treat it as a local prototype. There is no live iMessage account, general transposition engine, voice processing or guarantee about a singer's range on this trial.
