# Big Buck Bunny — Sacha Goedegebure / Blender Foundation

Picture samples at 46.60, 47.35, 47.60, 47.85 and 48.10 in the MediaElement excerpt.

- **Before.** The rabbit's ears and head rise at the mouth of the burrow. Its body is still mostly in shadow. The space it will move into is largely out of frame.
- **Choice.** The close view catches the shoulder and paw beginning to lift. The last close frame at 47.67 leads into the first wide frame at 47.71, which picks up the rabbit dipping forward and bracing on the rock as the same emergence continues.
- **After.** The viewer sees a single exit and also learns the size of the rabbit relative to the burrow and the clearing.
- **Intent guess.** The position change and shared body motion make a large jump in camera scale easy to follow.
- **Alternative.** Reveal the clearing while the rabbit is stationary. The geography arrives earlier, and the second setup would have to generate its own forward motion.
- **Change for Direction.** Where the viewer has to follow one physical action across a cut, choose a body or object movement already under way as the edit point. Check consecutive frames on both sides for direction and trajectory, especially at a scale change. Try this at a reach or a lift in the Keiki cut when the shots support it.
