# Weekly Bitcoin Rewards — Cash App

Picture samples at 06.00, 07.16, 07.74, 08.32, 08.90, 10.64 and 11.80.

- **Before.** The Bitcoin bonus toggle is on in the app. The audience has seen a preference set and has yet to see anything happen outside that screen.
- **Choice.** The same phone changes to a home screen and holds briefly without the app showing. A banner starts to appear near the top at 08.72. The edit later returns to the app.
- **After.** The viewer can infer that an event reaches the person through the device outside the settings flow. The short clean hold gives the banner an arrival the audience can perceive.
- **Intent guess.** The state separation makes an external notification read as an event rather than another settings value.
- **Alternative.** Leave the app up and put the reward information in a second row. More detail could remain visible, and the benefit of arriving outside the app would be hard to establish.
- **Change for Direction.** If the claim depends on the channel where an event lands, show the relevant prior state long enough to establish it. A benefit that is always visible in a composite frame may never read as something that happened. Use the channel's actual affordance for the distinct change.
