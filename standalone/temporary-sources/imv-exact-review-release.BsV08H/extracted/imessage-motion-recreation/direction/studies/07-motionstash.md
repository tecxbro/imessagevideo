# They Remember — Motionstash Studio

Picture samples at 14.90, 15.70, 16.10, 16.90 and 17.30.

- **Before.** The white eyes with dark pupils are central in a wide gray field. Their small glances are the only clear source of attention.
- **Choice.** The eyes rotate around 15.95 and lead the gaze into a blue-and-green image at 16.00. The scenery changes fully while the eyes retain the same rough scale and area of the frame. They react again at 17.30.
- **After.** The viewer can continue to look at the same pair of eyes, and understands the next image as a place those eyes have arrived or woken up in.
- **Intent guess.** The layout reserves enough visual continuity for a jump between environments that otherwise share little.
- **Alternative.** Establish the new landscape on its own before the eyes return. The viewer would see the setting sooner and have to find the active subject again.
- **Change for Direction.** On transitions that change a lot at once, identify exactly where the viewer's gaze lands in the first frame of the new composition. Preserve a single position, direction or recognizable feature if the cut needs instant orientation. Test the intended landing point in consecutive frames.
