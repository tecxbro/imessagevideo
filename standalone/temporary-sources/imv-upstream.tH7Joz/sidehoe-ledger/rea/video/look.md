## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium shot 55%, medium close-up 45%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 100%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: high key (bright overall, almost no shadows). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), at subject height; lighting ratio about 1.0:1 (0.1 stops); soft light (almost no lit / shadow edge); colour temperature cool (daylight / cool-white LED); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- (not live footage: the above describes the light in the picture, for generation prompts, not a shooting plan)
- Grade: black and white; near monochrome, low contrast, lifted blacks (greyish shadows), overall cool
- Editing: 15.4 cuts per minute, 3.46s average shot; no live-action shots
