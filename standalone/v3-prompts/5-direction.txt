YOUR BUILD
Build S/direction/SKILL.md by making a real film with it.

Your brief: a short original launch film for Keiki, for someone who has built an agent and wants customers to reach it through iMessage. Show one real Keiki capability in action. The concept, world, cast, pace, choreography, sound and ending are yours. Check every product claim against https://onkeiki.com/docs, /docs/channels and /docs/imessage-apps; save the URL, date and exact claim. Drop a claim you cannot support.

Start from what the product does and who feels the result. Develop more than one creative possibility where the choice matters, and build the strongest. Get a moving cut early, built around a visible action that proves the claim. Find the weakest decision and change it. If it feels like slides, find the exact moment the viewer loses a reason to follow the next shot and re-cut from there. Show at least one revision that changed the film, at specific frames. Use Motion's library or invent a movement, in whichever renderer the idea calls for. Finish it with Sound's mix.

While you build, take apart ten films from different makers. For one choice in each: what the audience knew just before, what the filmmaker changed, what the audience could infer after, and what a different choice would have done. Your reading of intent is a guess; label it. Each film should change your cut or the skill. Keep the ten studies in their own folder, loaded only on request, so a future director starts from a blank brief without their style or cost.

Write the skill around the decisions a director makes, not a fixed order of shots.

Then it is tested without you: Observer gives a fresh agent a second, different brief with your skill and without your film. If it repeats your premise, opening or structure, find the instruction that taught imitation and change it.

Deliver the skill, the editable project, the finished film with its mix, the revision evidence and the second-brief result.
