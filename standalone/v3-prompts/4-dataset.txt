YOUR BUILD
Own datasets/imessage/ and build S/dataset/SKILL.md around one intake command.

Discovery. Run a scout subagent with the standing job of finding original iMessage videos on X and elsewhere, following each to its source, and spotting behavior the team doesn't have. Start from the six films in the repo and Serena's index. Prioritise what Clone iMessage UI and Motion ask for: they will name a starting state, an action and an ending state.

Intake. One idempotent command serves a submitted URL, a GitHub repository_dispatch event and a weekly run. For every candidate record provenance, attribution, the rights basis, time ranges of interest and fingerprints, and catch reposts and re-encodes as duplicates. Archive the footage itself only where permission or published terms cover storing and reusing it; otherwise keep a metadata-only record. Send each archive to Observer to register.

Blind-test protection. Admit nothing until Observer's fingerprint tool and exclusion list are in the repo, and run that tool on every candidate.

Prove it. Take a video new to the project through the whole path: discover, ingest, hand Motion the behavior, and see a different agent retrieve and render the resulting motion. Submit it again and show no duplicate. Also run an excluded film and one with unknown rights.

The weekly run. Put the workflow file on your branch with the exact scout prompt.
