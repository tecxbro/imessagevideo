Every teammate's film renders through what you build.

YOUR BUILD
You own the breadth of this interface. Discover its surfaces, states, transitions, content types and the relationships between them from the real Messages app, native recordings, existing implementations and footage. Build your own coverage map. Anything a source or teammate names is a starting point; keep looking for behavior the rest of us have missed.

Build S/ui/SKILL.md, a component kit another agent can use, the contract for content and state, and one adapter that turns a described conversation into a scene the repo's renderers can draw.

Make an architecture decision that serves the whole repo. The sources give you working React/TypeScript components and the repo renders through five routes. Decide what stays a native component, what becomes shared layout and state data, and where an adapter is worth having. Identity and timing stay the same across renderers. Keep one implementation the whole team imports, never five drifting copies. Back each renderer you claim with a real moving export.

Three teammates are waiting, so ship a thin working adapter early and grow it from what breaks.

Build against moving evidence.
- Match the device, OS version and scale for each case.
- Look at what happens before, during and after an action.
- Track which object owns each change and what stays attached under scrolling and camera movement.
- Check how layout responds to content you didn't plan for, and which state persists.
- A good still proves only the state it shows. Render the whole transition and change the content to expose clipping, reflow and identity defects.
- In the coverage map, keep verified behavior, approximations and missing evidence apart.
- When evidence is missing, ask Dataset for the capture and keep building what doesn't depend on it.

Then have a fresh agent, with only your skill, build an interaction you didn't plan for, change its content and render again. Wherever it redraws something, hard-codes a layout or fakes a state, fix the kit.

Deliver the kit, the content and state contract, an editable conversation, moving comparisons against native evidence, and the coverage map.
