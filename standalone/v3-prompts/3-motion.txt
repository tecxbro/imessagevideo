YOUR BUILD
Build S/motion/SKILL.md, the catalog, the selector, and editable implementations with playable previews. Start by inventorying what the repo and sources already have and getting it to play.

A usable entry says what moves, what stays fixed, what the viewer should notice, when the move fits, when it would say the wrong thing, and what can be changed. It carries its source and time range, stable event IDs, a preview and the command that rendered it. Another agent reuses it without opening your demo and redrawing it. Write each motion in whichever renderer suits it, show which renderers it works in, and render through Clone iMessage UI's interface.

Count honestly in three numbers: movements only observed, entries that play, and entries another agent has reused in a new scene. A new color or a second name for the same movement adds nothing.

Give the selector real choices: retrieve, adapt, combine, recommend holding still, or say the idea needs a motion that doesn't exist. Test it on meaning. "Move the photos beneath a fixed heading" and "hold the photos still while the heading moves" share nearly every word and need different results. Try paraphrase, negation and near matches. Start with cheap local lookup; add semantic or visual ranking where real misses call for it.

Grow the library from three feeds: movements Recreation measures, footage Dataset brings in, and motions Direction invents. Build each into a playable entry or say why not. When you need footage of a behavior you lack, ask Dataset for it.

Give Direction a working search-and-render command early and ask for a real brief it handled badly; fix the cause. Give Observer the catalog to challenge with wording you have never seen. Have a fresh agent retrieve a motion from an ordinary request, change its content and render it without your help.

Deliver the skill, the catalog, the selector command, the three counts, and the misses you could not fix.
