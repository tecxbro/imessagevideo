## Motion findings and an isolation test

**The phones drift sideways because their keyframes move the horizontal center.** In frames 323–331 and 641–649 the original rim center spans 1 and 0 coded pixels, respectively. The recreated center spans 10 pixels in both. The authored side positions and scale jointly produce that swing. I changed only the horizontal center in a temporary diagnostic copy, kept the same scales, vertical positions, output resolution, renderer and encoder, then decoded both nine-frame tests. The center spans 2 pixels in both tests. The rim width still varies, so that experiment establishes the cause of the horizontal wobble only. [Isolation measurements](center-isolation.csv) · [First handset edge measurements](sofia-rim.csv) · [Second handset edge measurements](follow-rim.csv)

**The pointer has three distinct motion errors.** On the detached Send? view the original dark silhouette grows from 8,516 pixels at frame 385 to 11,729 at 391, then drops to 5,458 at 396. The recreation stays near 5,300 throughout. At the follow-up track switch from 673 to 674, the original pointer tip moves about 21 coded pixels and the recreation about 40. During withdrawal at 691→692, the original tip moves down by 3 coded pixels while the recreation moves up by 19. These measurements use the dominant dark pointer component in each native RGB crop. The reproduced contact at frame 675 can coexist with errors on both sides.
