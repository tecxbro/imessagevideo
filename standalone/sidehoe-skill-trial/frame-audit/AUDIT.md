# Sidehoe frame-by-frame audit

**The reconstruction is visibly different.** I compared the original movie and the latest editable render at all 778 decoded frame positions. The presentation starts and ends match exactly. Every pair differs at the native RGB level, including frame 777, where the largest channel difference is one level. Both endings look black and hold for 40 ms. The earlier black-frame check used a downsampled image.

## What caused the misses

The earlier skill already required measuring visible movements and passing visual probes. I left important source observations as descriptions, then filled those gaps with shared curves and assets. That explains most of the visual misses. The skill also had a gap. A short export and a correctly named event could count as evidence without a check that the outgoing and incoming layers were actually visible on the right frames. The missing suggestion and calendar prove the latter problem.

The worst frame is **690**, with native mean RGB error **26.57**. The handset edges at mid-height measure approximately **232–1764** in the original and **200–1800** in the render, an extra **68 coded pixels of width**. That estimate comes from the dark rim at 500 px inspection scale, converted to coded pixels. The original has already started to narrow. The candidate is still near its held width. At 692 the edges converge, then diverge again on the final retreat. The camera keyframes and the separate y correction explain the modeled motion. I have not isolated how much of the total frame error comes from motion, blur or the in-screen details. ## Repairs tested and skill changes

I tested the two confirmed onset defects on copied editable scenes. The revision restored a visible suggestion at frame 336 and visible calendar art at 397, with the preceding test frames still clear. In the fixed crops the measured color-pixel counts change from **0 to 2,107** for the suggestion and **0 to 12,698** for the calendar. The originals have **1,660** and **16,995** respectively at that inspection scale. These are tests of visible onset. The geometry and shading still differ and remain open.

[Suggestion test](onset-proof/suggestion.jpg) · [Calendar test](onset-proof/calendar.jpg) · [Measurements](onset-proof/observations.json)

The installed skill now calls for last absent, first visible and first settled evidence at clip boundaries; per-element timing and z-order in composite transitions; and an evidence-backed diagnosis with an explicit repair test for each failed shot. The reusable checker now has an opt-in `--pairs` export that opens every aligned frame in a viewer. It reports exact pixel equality independently from visual acceptance.
