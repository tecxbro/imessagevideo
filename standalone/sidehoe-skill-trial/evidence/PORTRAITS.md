# Portrait asset provenance

Both atlases were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The first used
text. The second used the supplied film's portrait reference grid as guidance.
The selected sprites are in the editable project under `assets/portraits`.
Their background disks, positions, expressions, masks and movement are authored
separately. Inspect the rendered probes at shot scale.

## First atlas

> Production sprite atlas with transparent background. One exact 4-column by 3-row regular grid of twelve distinct 3D sculpted cartoon avatar head cutouts, centered in each cell with generous clear gutters. No text, labels, badges, circles, shadows on the background, shoulders or shirts. Front-facing head including all hair; hair may hang down around a very short neck but never reaches a neighboring cell. High-end soft key light from upper left, slight warm cheek tint, expressive large brown eyes, smooth rounded clay/plastic shading, real rounded facial anatomy, strong readable silhouette, likeness among matching variant heads. Bottom framing at the neck or lower hair, no torso. Consistent visual scale and placement at every grid cell, each head about 66 percent of the cell width and 75 percent height.
> Top row left to right: Marie light caramel skin with brunette braids and subtle round glasses smiling; Sydney tawny blond bob, pale warm complexion, sad face; Darla medium tan skin, dark blue-black wavy hair, dark eyeglasses; Eleanor mahogany skin with short warm brown waves and eyeglasses.
> Middle row: Ella pale warm complexion, long swept golden blonde hair and an open friendly smile; Margot light peach skin with hazelnut-brown wavy hair and a slightly sideways smile; Danielle warm tan skin, long chestnut hair, laughing open mouth; Lisa light peach skin, long center-parted espresso brown hair, smiling with both eyes open.
> Bottom row: Maria pale warm skin with honey-blond loose waves over one cheek, small smile; Rae brown skin with dark braids and large glossy cherry red sunglasses; same Lisa character with identical hair and face but winking and a small tongue-out grin; a spare auburn-haired person. Maintain exact count and fixed row order. No background marks.

## Selected reference-guided atlas

> Create a clean high-resolution transparent production atlas from the provided authorized avatar reference grid. Preserve its 4 columns by 3 rows and same character order. Each cell contains a newly rendered soft-lit 3D avatar head, matching the corresponding reference cell's hair silhouette, facial proportions, facial expression, accessories, hair color, skin color and simplified material. Use the source grid as an identity and silhouette guide. Make the hair simple sculpted shapes with small highlights of the supplied reference, no realistic curled strands or glossy doll look. No shoulders. Center each head horizontally and vertically at about 66% of cell width/75% of cell height. Keep a generous transparent margin around each head. Remove all circular colored portrait backings, white areas, chips, text, backdrop and other elements. Only the head, hair and its facial accessories. For row 1 column 2 remove the text overlapping the blonde hair. For row 1 column 3 remove the floating Z symbols, keeping the sleepy face and blue glasses. In row 1 column 4 retain the gold glasses and visible peace-sign hand from the source. For row 2 column 4 use the brunette with both eyes open as the alternate; row 3 column 3 keep her winking with tongue out. Leave row 3 column 4 empty. Exact quadrant separation. Genuine full alpha transparency.
