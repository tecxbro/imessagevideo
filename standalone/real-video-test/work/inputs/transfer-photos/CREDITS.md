# Cedar Pass Lodge test photographs

The lodge heading is fictional. The photos depict Rocky Mountain National Park. These four photos are public-domain U.S. government materials and were center-cropped and resized for the transfer test.

- [Bear Lake](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bear_Lake_(44447883222).jpg), National Park Service photograph by M. Reed.
- [Graced by Wildflowers](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trail_Ridge_Road-Beaver_Meadow_Road_-_Graced_by_Wildflowers_-_NARA_-_7722570.jpg), Rocky Mountain National Park / National Archives (NAID 7722570).
- [Snowy Slopes of the Rocky Mountains](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trail_Ridge_Road-Beaver_Meadow_Road_-_Snowy_Slopes_of_the_Rocky_Mountains_-_NARA_-_7722538.jpg), National Park Service / National Archives (NAID 7722538).
- [Path to Forest Canyon Overlook](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trail_Ridge_Road-Beaver_Meadow_Road_-_Path_to_Forest_Canyon_Overlook_-_NARA_-_7722550.jpg), photo by Sally Pearce, National Archives (NAID 7722550).
