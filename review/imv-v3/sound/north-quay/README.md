# North Quay count sound study

This is a separate scoring study on Motion's silent North Quay picture from `37b72e8`. Its [event map](picture.events.json) comes from the same pin. Picture SHA-256 is `d5e367b02fac37c9918696604444b41599a434e7300ed810d8a7c780b36352d1`. Neither pending film is represented here. The [adjacent frame evidence](anchor-frames.png) reads 620→621 at f9→10 and 1,278→1,280 at f54→55.

| Treatment | First count at f10 | Final value at f55 | MP4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A, tactile | Paper tick impact at −8 ms | Felt hit at +16 ms, then a quiet hold | [Play A](a/mix.mp4) |
| B, tonal | Warm tonal rise impact at +8 ms | An open fifth at +26 ms, then a quiet hold | [Play B](b/mix.mp4) |

Both leave the first number in silence and score two visible events. Look for whether the final number feels settled and still has enough room to read. A and B measure −33.1 and −32.3 LUFS integrated, with peak estimates −9.87 and −17.88 dBTP. Both decoded cue checks measure 0.000 ms error from their intended offsets. Human audition is pending. A serves as the timing-control baseline.

Each treatment has an editable JSON plan, its original synthesized WAV sources under [assets](assets/), rendered bus and individual cue stems, PCM master, picture-bound manifest and decoded verification report in its output folder. The supplied synthesis recipe uses seeded noise and damped resonances for A and slightly bending oscillator pairs for B. The pitch bend gives the tonal cue a distinctive attack that survives decoding. A constant-pitch version produced an ambiguous waveform match.

## Variable frame rate

A [synthetic timing revision](variable-picture.mp4) retains all 72 decoded frames, pixel for pixel, and the exact 2.4-second runtime. Three different display intervals delay the first frame of the held total by 200 ms. The value still appears first on f55. On the original the display clock puts it at sample 88,000. On the revision it resolves to sample 97,600. The authored +768-sample impact offset carries through unchanged, from sample 88,768 to 98,368.

The [revised A mux](variable-a/mix.mp4) preserves the variant's picture packets and PTS and passes at 0.000 ms error. [Reusing the original A mix](variable-stale.verify.json) fails with the confirmation 200.000 ms early, while the paper tick still passes. ## Reproduce the score and timing checks

Run from the repository root.

```sh
S=skill/imessage-motion-recreation/sound
D=review/imv-v3/sound/north-quay
work=$(mktemp -d)
(cd "$D" && sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS)
python3 "$S/north-quay/synthesize.py" "$work/assets"
for take in a b variable-a; do
  plan="$D/$take.json"
  picture="$D/picture.mp4"
  if [ "$take" = variable-a ]; then
    plan="$D/a.json"
    picture="$D/variable-picture.mp4"
  fi
  python3 "$S/sound.py" mix "$plan" "$picture" "$work/$take"
  PYTHONPATH="$S" python3 -m recreation.render_stems "$plan" "$work/$take"
  python3 "$S/sound.py" verify "$plan" "$picture" "$work/$take/mix.mp4" \
    "$work/$take/manifest.json" --report "$work/$take/verify.json"
done
status=0
python3 "$S/retime_control.py" "$D/a.json" "$D/variable-picture.mp4" \
  "$work/a/manifest.json" "$work/stale" || status=$?
test "$status" -eq 1
```

The variable picture was generated by setting PTS in 1/30,000-second ticks. The added 6,000 ticks drain away over the final hold. The last packet has its explicit 1,000-tick display duration.

```sh
ffmpeg -nostdin -v error -y -i "$D/picture.mp4" -map 0:v:0 -an \
  -vf "settb=1/30000,setpts='1000*N+200*clip(N-20,0,30)-400*clip(N-55,0,15)'" \
  -fps_mode passthrough -enc_time_base 1:30000 -video_track_timescale 30000 \
  -c:v libx264 -qp 0 -bf 0 -threads:v 2 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -bsf:v "setts=duration=if(eq(N\,71)\,1000\,DURATION)" "$work/variable-picture.mp4"
```
