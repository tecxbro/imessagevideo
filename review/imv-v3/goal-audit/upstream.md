## Where each upstream workflow helps

**REA Video finds source evidence.** Keep its layered `VIDEO.md` package, shot contact sheets, machine classifications, human evidence, and rhythm and transition notes. Its documentation asks a visual agent to see the frames and leave unknown shots open. A split suggestion annotates a shot without changing its boundaries. The current local command runs deterministic decomposition and review, then maps REA’s exclusive ends to native inclusive frame ranges and stores the measured presentation timestamps. It doesn’t run the optional upstream survey route. Its renderer field recommends clock and geometry eligibility. Treatment similarity needs its own probes. **HyperFrames `reverse-any-video` is a useful measurement recipe.** Carry over source resolution, rational rate and measured changes; sample consecutive frames around motion onset, cut and settle; extract ink bounds and spacing; hold hard text replacements; match zoom origin and directional blur. Support regional before, transition and after probes and use evaluator → lossless render → encoded export to localize defects. Upstream `measure curve` is for isolated moving content on a simple background. A handset, cursor, text and blur overlapping during camera motion need explicit tracks with layer and viewport coordinates. Use REA’s code remake output as an analysis or comparison scratch asset when relevant. Its extracted sprites, text overlays and source placeholders do not form an authored, editable Messages interaction. ## Decision layer to verify against real jobs

| User intent | Route and first proof |
| --- | --- |
| Rebuild the supplied film, as with Sidehoe | **Recreation.** Decode and lock the source clock. Use REA and reverse-any-video to produce candidates for cuts, tracks and treatment, then resolve the most important disagreement in native frames. Show the hardest sourced iMessage passage with its independently authored, editable UI; preserve the surrounding visual form and ending wherever fidelity is requested. A motion preset or different renderer is a candidate implementation only after it matches the same in-context probes. |
| Make or change an iMessage action or treatment | **Motion.** Resolve the requested component, starting and ending states, preserve list and output type. Choose a genuinely playable catalog entry by action and layer, or author a measured track if the action is absent. Review before, onset and settle in a mounted Messages scene. On an existing film preserve its renderer and neighboring behavior where feasible. |
| Make an original iMessage film, as with a new Pinch Hit | **Director.** Verify the underlying claim, then express its setup, request, reply, action and legible payoff in a Messages sequence. Start from an editable Messages project and exercise the actual causal passage before visual embellishment. Use HyperFrames story and cut alternatives inside that constraint. Test with titles muted as a diagnostic so a detached headline cannot provide the missing action, while keeping relevant labels and real copy in the UI. |
| Turn a non-iMessage film into an iMessage film | **Director adaptation** when the requested deliverable is a transformed film. Log the source traits the user wants fixed, such as narrative, rhythm or a move, and map them to named in-thread states, actors and interactions. Use REA/reverse to source the evidence for the carried traits and Motion for isolated interactions. Review both correspondence to those retained traits and causal clarity in the new conversation. Where the only requested result is one adapted motion, use Motion. Reserve an exact Recreation claim for the job whose original image and timing are the fidelity target. |

Run the decision layer against actual positive and negative clips. Keep Sidehoe as the exact source target and test its first complex passage from onset through settle. Keep the rejected Pinch Hit as a failure control, along with frozen chat under a camera move and slide-like headlines surrounding decorative bubbles. A green technical check must still leave those editorial criteria open. Use the editable Messages send as a technical positive and require a separate native-looking, iMessage-led Director passage with a continuous action and payoff. Have a reviewer explain from frames who acted, what changed and where the result appeared. Tie every result to the candidate bytes. The router receives an explicit desired change and delivery goal before looking at upstream tools.
