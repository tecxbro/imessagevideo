# Production case 06 · Rene / Gallery

Rene establishes how to recover a complex Messages scene from a supplied reference. Its strongest results came from measuring and repairing the handset, header, conversation, composer, keyboard and obscuring artwork independently. The frozen project carries detailed descriptions of those layers, but a large share of its visible UI depends on traced, rasterized, or per-frame components. Its renderer and review methods are reusable. Its source-specific assets, thresholds, patch chain, and text treatments need to stay with the historical case.

## What carries over to the iMessage skill

1. **Keep timing on the displayed source frame clock.** Persist decoded source indices and PTS, an explicit output map, measured first visible action, last visible action, and exclusive ends. Confirm cut neighbors from the encoded candidate. Rene needed a narrow repair for float roundoff at five crossings. Some old measurement scripts also seek by timestamp and enumerate at nominal 30 fps. Those historical assumptions should not drive the clock of a new reference. [Boundary repair](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/06-gallery-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/06-gallery/canonicalize_frame_boundaries.py)
2. **Track and review real component ownership.** Use a common camera for the physical phone where proven. Measure header, transcript, composer, keyboard, each entering message, and any overlapping prop within their own apertures. Registration should show actor identity, parent transform and mutable channels, plus whether offscreen or occluded samples are observed, fitted, or inferred. The f532 and birthday cases demonstrate what a plausible aggregate similarity score can hide. This transfers directly to Sidehoe's phone edges, cursor tip, and calendar tile measurements.
3. **Preserve the editability the user asked for.** The frozen scene has 4,011 unique IDs, 1,259 keyed nodes, 1,698 image instances resolving to 659 paths, 771 paths, 71 live text nodes, and 1,226 semantic labels. Its five fonts and all referenced assets resolved locally. That inventory includes hidden legacy text, repeated per-frame actors, and 30 repeated clock/back/name nodes in the phone tail. There is no conversation event timeline or cue map. Keep a stable conversation event, composable UI hierarchy and live glyph source for new Motion projects. Treat bounded textures, traced glyphs, rasterized optical effects, and reused source audio as separately disclosed recreation choices. No evidence from the frozen case establishes that modifying a Messages interaction updates a native sound event.
4. **Prove the patch and the picture independently.** Check scene/base SHA, asset content, actor IDs, declared frame union, unaffected root ordering, and unchanged evaluated output across every frame outside the union. The best frozen composers also preserve prior lossless frames, publish only the one source row under a lock, and leave the encoded gate pending until the next review. Require exact candidate and source identities plus native crops/gettable controls for any acceptance. The CSS/GSAP or other renderer should consume the authored scene and emit independently reviewable output. ## Durability boundaries

- The portable frozen scene resolves its dependencies. Most measurement writers require historical `/workspace/imessage-recreations` paths, untracked intermediate snapshots/decoded PNGs, private scratch dependency paths, and a sibling project's tracing helper. Earlier scripts also edit the old canonical JSON directly or silently use fallback payloads. Final releases use stronger gates, but rerunning the 23 historical revisions from the archive remains unverified.
- A few crop labels assert strict source ownership while the code uses a mask expanded around an expected pose, contains unapertured header crop interiors, or fits source colors to measured optical shapes. These are localized techniques for the documented reference. Asset reuse for a new conversation should depend on actual permitted source material, inspectable alpha contents, and the editability required for the new project. The original lock screen also uses bounded bitmap interiors. It cannot prove a generic portrait or Messages motion recipe.
