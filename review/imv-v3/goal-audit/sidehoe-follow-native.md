## Scoped trajectory test

A scratch probe substitutes the source's observed stage-space tip on f674–694 at the final cursor call, accounting for where the baseline raster tip falls below its evaluated model pivot. It leaves the pointer asset, scale, angle, opacity and the rest of the shot in place. Before the substitution, the traced renderer reproduces the entire f672–697 original lossless pixel sequence using the Sidehoe Python environment. A different environment with the same Pillow version failed the f672 PNG equality check. Lock the actual baseline runtime for this comparison.

Across 21 target frames the mean tip error drops from 10.05 to 0.87 coded px, and the worst drops from 56.44 to 1.80 px. After the same full-size H.264 encode settings, using a new short clip with frame 0 mapped to f673, the mean is 1.02 px and the worst 2.50 px. Mean pointer-neighborhood RGB error drops from 46.90 to 37.56 before encode. Nineteen frames improve, f678 is identical after placement rounding, and f679 regresses from 35.36 to 38.45. The crop includes the unchanged Yes material and the different arrow silhouette. At f693, the corrected tip follows the source but visibly separates from the still-misplaced Yes chip. This is evidence for separate chip, pointer and optics corrections. It leaves the candidate below shot acceptance. The native probe pixels stay identical at controls f673, f695 and f696.
