## Why the old path drifted

The Director entry offered a blank video project and treated Messages as
optional. Pinch Hit used repeated headline and prop compositions. Its
technical checks passed without evidence of a send, response or in-thread
payoff. Motion's catalog also packaged generic demonstration frames through a
shared heading, card and footer. One mixed `--ui` command cut from a Messages
thread into a standalone metric card. The router sent requests for motion
options or an original using a reference's style to Recreation. These are
workflow and authoring failures. [Source and checks](upstream.md#how-pinch-hit-became-a-presentation) ·
[Motion failure probes](motion/AUDIT.md#failure-paths-and-smallest-repair)

Five renderers reproduced a different authored-state error on Poke. They all
show a restaurant row selected at f645, while the decoded source shows
selection at f633. The frozen scene schedules it at 21.5 seconds. This is
why checking a real interaction across adjacent frames earns separate
acceptance from renderer parity. [Poke native checks](production-04.md)

## Editable project proof

The portable HyperFrames project contains a single 114-frame Messages
conversation at 24 fps. The phone, conversation and event IDs stay editable.
I checked consecutive decoded pictures around send f38, typing f56, and
reply f88. The send cue was independently located in the final AAC at
sample 76,000 with 0.0 ms measured impact error. The clean archive reopened
and rerendered the silent picture byte for byte. I then changed the draft to
“Meet me at the blue doors.” and moved the send by one frame in an extracted
copy. The decoded picture put the send at f39 and the checked AAC impact
moved to sample 78,000 with 0.0 ms measured error. [Picture and audio
contract](../../../skill/imessage-motion-recreation/contracts/README.md)

The binding checks reject a send that instantly lands, a send whose settle
extends beyond the film, a page jump presented as a slide, a fixed title
moving inside its header, a reply reflow without prior rows moving, a fixed
composer moving during the selected typing onset, a preview request for an
unavailable alternate adapter and a mixed UI export containing a standalone
metric card. Each portable project starts from a conversation and declares
its approved appearance. Director can use a sequence only after the selected
bindings and their actual pictures are proven.

The latest Recreation suite passed all 25 expected outcomes, including an
exact identity control and rejections for one changed pixel, late selection,
partial or unrelated evidence, and a stale comparison. Train Options passes
the published regional thresholds on all 120 frames. Its stricter
pixel-equality check remains a failure, at mean RGB error 0.0603.
