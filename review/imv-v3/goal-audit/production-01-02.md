## PhotonHQ reconstruction decisions to retain

- **Match visual identity, scale and lifetime independently.** Early generic foreground and row fades merged different cards and ghosted the source’s staggered flips. The semantic tracker separates color/material bodies, inverts white-on-color ink for OCR, identifies a card by both caption and local trajectory, and chooses its most complete unobscured asset. Two identical captions could otherwise cross rows. Neighboring positions and an independently fitted visible opacity provide evidence; an OCR miss does not establish a disappearance. This applies to Sidehoe’s component masks and text identity, with manual anchors when font/blurring defeats OCR. [Component masks and OCR](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/track-wall-ocr.py#L11-L56) · [Trajectory assignment](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/track-wall-ocr.py#L115-L146) · [Work log](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/steps-rebuild.md#L33-L48)
- **Measure full poses through clipping.** The clipping fix estimates camera scale from visible controls and anchors the full artwork to the observable opposite edge. The cursor fit separates translation, scale and rotation, and clearly marks the first clipped tip’s unseen scale and turn as inferred from its neighbor. The pill press is its own independently measured material, with the occluded region explicitly inferred. Sidehoe needs those same distinctions for projected handset corners, cursor tip and press/release through the entire move. [Clipping correction](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/fix-clipped-card-transforms.py#L9-L33) · [Cursor and pressed material](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/refine-intro-cursor-press.py#L15-L52)
- **Prove sampling, source use and the exception boundary.** A nominal 10 Hz FFmpeg contact set sampled native frame `3n+1` rather than `3n`. Align actual decoded indices before measuring source/candidate geometry. A 36-frame exception uses independently bounded, frame-local textures for severely deformed card art, with an exposed category and provenance; surrounding text outlines and blur motion remain separate. The final scene counts show why this route gains source fidelity at the expense of editable copy. Do not carry historical source-pixel exceptions into Sidehoe’s independent-artwork constraint. Verify glyph and tile material independently. [Frame offset and local deformation evidence](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/steps-rebuild.md#L41-L48) · [Local deformation implementation](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/01-photonhq-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/01-photonhq/refine-local-flips.py#L19-L42)
- **Keep a repair scoped when more replacement makes it worse.** The accepted alpha repair verifies unchanged RGB, counts altered pixels and keeps the revised SMS card visible only on improving frames. Its continuous variant is retained under a rejected name. The V9 mail path represents measured affine content registration as nested rotations/scales on an existing isolated asset, with hard visibility spans and the valid frame 300 explicitly preserved. The same mechanism can test whether Sidehoe’s calendar needs a new asset or a different transform and mask. ## Daniel Tian reconstruction decisions to retain

- **Separate interface objects from views of those objects.** Original phonebook glyphs change their own spacing and opacity as the field moves. The seven-row spacing changed around 1.20× while glyph widths changed around 1.04×. Consecutive exact frames distinguished approximate uniform scale, clipping and true selection. The fast phone exit independently measures the parent camera, relative outgoing and reply poses, and a world-fixed white fade. That directly applies to Sidehoe’s screen content versus handset motion versus stage blur. [Phonebook diagnosis](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/02-danieltian-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/02-danieltian/steps-phonebook.md#L3-L24) · [Independent phone exit](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/02-danieltian-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/02-danieltian/refine-v11-phone-exit.py#L14-L21)
- **Separate material, controls, copy and birth.** The composer field estimates its neutral RGB underlay from samples excluding ink, caret and control. The authoring proof found pale blue material that a grayscale model lost. A subsequent five-frame send-flight patch carried the original line width and actual moving UI into the next beat, improved each frame and preserved adjacent controls. The query fade found optical banding and ultimately used one separated UI material with measured camera, blur and opacity. A header prototype that improved one frame and regressed others was rejected. Sidehoe’s detached “31” glyph, tile face, shadow and camera should receive similarly independent tests. [Composer/copy, send flight and material experiments](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/02-danieltian-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/02-danieltian/steps-root-copy.md#L3-L32) · [Header material gate](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/02-danieltian-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/02-danieltian/steps-root-final.md#L3-L9)
- **Look for simultaneous actors and retain exact valid endpoints.** Two overlapping avatars coexist through each of three thread changes. One track with moving rows omitted the fading actor. V19 independently fits both opacities, preserves original camera and image bytes, fits the cropped birth using the observed full-size actor, creates separately timed clones and adds hold guards so interpolation cannot leak into accepted frames. Sidehoe should likewise separate outgoing and incoming components in the calendar reveal. A renderer can accurately draw an incorrect authored lifecycle. [Fit and clipping](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/02-danieltian-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/02-danieltian/muse-continuation-v19/measure-overlaps.py#L7-L22) · [Scoped apply](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/02-danieltian-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/02-danieltian/muse-continuation-v19/apply.py#L14-L31)

Its native source gate checks source/baseline/candidate, exact adjacent guards and unchanged pixels outside the avatar ROIs. The final encoded source scan compared every frame and disclosed residual errors including glass, tails, material and AA. The Remotion/GSAP comparison documented sparse optical differences. Its low MAE is a renderer-parity measurement. SVG equality, native guard equality and full-mux RGB source equality are separately reportable. ## Sidehoe transfer order

1. Lock the decoded source map. For each target native frame, record semantic identity, visible support, clipping/occlusion, full inferred pose, camera-space and stage-space coordinates, and uncertainty separately. Inspect the cut/reveal neighborhood before classifying it.
2. Probe the source’s most problematic resident and moving states on one shared independently authored scene. Keep the calendar glyph, face and material, cursor tip and handset edges separately addressable. Compare measured source → evaluated scene → lossless native render → final mux. Prove the transition start and movement and the neighboring controls, alongside the hold.
3. Select a different renderer only when a correct evaluated state produces a material or compositing error in the current one and the second renderer improves the same native controls without changing the authored scene. Preserve the existing baseline, source and candidate hashes, exact scope and full encoded scan before promotion. These packs use tested primitives across five engines, plus narrow adapters and explicit asset choices, so a cross-renderer match cannot establish source correctness by itself.
