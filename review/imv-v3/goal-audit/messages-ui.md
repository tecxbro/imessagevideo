# Messages interface audit

21 November 2026. The tagged skill has an editable conversation and event model worth repairing. Its two film skins deliberately form their own visual language. Sidehoe bypasses them and draws its own hardware, keyboard, portraits, and symbols. The existing fidelity guide asks for the right measurements, but the execution did not turn those measurements into a reusable screen or require its locked component comparisons before a full render. Sidehoe therefore exposes missing artwork, measurement, and authoring decisions. These results do not demonstrate a fundamental Pillow limitation.

## First component to build and independent acceptance

Start with an editable **contact thread screen**. Extend the existing layout behind a selectable profile instead of duplicating transcript/event logic. Its contract declares coordinate space, device/appearance, service, text metrics, bubble path/tail/group rules, usable chrome, avatar slots, glyph resolution and proven keyboard variants. Keep the display mask and software chrome separate from physical handset hardware, lighting and the world transform. Drive all three from stable IDs so a camera move leaves layout unchanged. Sidehoe’s profile can override measured source colors and handset styling. Keep those overrides on Sidehoe. Require explicit project assets where there is no reusable rights-cleared equivalent.

Lock boxes for handset-top edges, selected incoming/outgoing body and tail, text, compose controls, letter baselines, emoji/dictation keys and home bar. Negative controls must fail for a missing or flipped tail, one-frame missing keyboard, substitution of generic blue for the measured cyan, and a displaced home bar. Hold out adjacent frames and a separately characterized conversation to catch overfitting the shot.
2. **Glyph and identity.** Hold f22, f435 and f670 as glyph regression targets and the f242 cluster as a separate identity check. Retain Unicode cluster, exact asset provenance, resolved strike/scale, baseline, bounding box and advance in the editable project. 3. **Independent device evidence.** For an iOS appearance profile, use an authorized capture of another conversation from a confirmed device/build, matched for scale, typography, service and appearance. 4. **Motion and continuity.** Isolate the panel’s evaluator and the handset/cursor track. For every frame of f313–335, f624–647 and f675–695, compare source points to evaluated edges and cursor tip, then lossless pixels, then final decoded pixels. A correct end pose does not close intermediate defects.
5. **Edit and export.** Open the clean scene, change one message and avatar, commit a send at a different frame, and validate held group layout, exact first visible frame, cue shift, and media paths. Repeat under the approved renderer(s) and record seek order, render scale and encode. The Motion catalog should consume this profile through declared component prerequisites. The most valuable next test is one slice containing the held f327 screen and adjacent phone movement. An independent build-matched capture is necessary to promote a general iOS profile. The results above justify repairing the component contract and review workflow now.
