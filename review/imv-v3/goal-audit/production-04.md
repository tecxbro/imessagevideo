## The restaurant selection arrives late in the scene

The source shows the blue Zareen’s check at native frame 633, 21.1211 seconds. Fresh GSAP, HTML, Remotion, Motion Canvas and Pillow renders from the frozen scene all show the unselected control at that frame. In the source, the half-open native crop `x=[1089,1145), y=[476,532)` contains 2,465 pixels meeting `B > 125, B - R > 60, G - R > 30` in decoded RGB. Each adapter’s still contains zero in that crop and zero anywhere at `x >= 900` under the same predicate. This is a local control-state observation backed by visual review. A fresh contiguous source decode of frames 620–645 locates the onset. The larger crop `x=[1040,1200), y=[290,620)` contains zero qualifying pixels through f632, then 2,490 at f633 with bounds `[1089,476,1146,533)` and roughly 2,485 per frame through f645.

Both blue-circle `p0249` and white-tick `p0250` in the frozen scene start at 21.5 seconds, so the first frame eligible to show the authored selection is 645. The 12-frame delay is present in the authored timeline and in all five delivered MP4s. A second native-index decode confirmed the published selection is absent from frames 632 through 644 and begins at 645 in every export. The first builder set those two starts directly. ## Fit and review methods to retain

- Derive source decode from native index and PTS. An early fps-filter fit used an inferred frame phase. The rebuild corrected its timestamp assumption after verifying the source uses a 1/3000000 time base and 100100 ticks per frame. An early sampled fit conflated frame 839 with 840. Same-frame comparison changed the recorded baseline. Use decoded frame identity rather than rounded annotations or project seconds when comparing to a source. [Early fallback](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/04-poke-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/04-poke/build_fitted_candidate.py#L4-L11) · [PTS correction](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/04-poke-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/04-poke/steps-rebuild.md#L29-L36) · [Frame-number correction](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/04-poke-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/04-poke/steps-rebuild.md#L40-L45)
- Separate camera, material, glyphs, clipping and interaction. The V19 Sarah pass registered neutral body edges for camera and blur, used a separate glyph-ink threshold for faint frames, retained independently ranked Text Regular, and gave the underline its own material and geometry. It preserved the visibility uncertainty of side arcs after the center was covered. This is directly transferable to Sidehoe handset pose, calendar tile material and glyph rendering. Historical source-derived material assets remain governed by their own provenance. [V19 build log](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/04-poke-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/04-poke/steps-rebuild.md#L142-L147)
- Register against an actual render control and reject identity confusion. A low-residual tile detector found the wrong neighboring tile. The V18 workflow retained the rejected observations, used a four-anchor consensus, verified the complete 48-frame probe, and limited the accepted change to frames 1170–1210 after confirming adjacent states. Sidehoe should keep source vs evaluated track, lossless render and encoded frame as distinct checks. [V18 build log](../../../../codex-direction-library/projects/04-poke-complete-v1/imessage-recreations/references/04-poke/steps-rebuild.md#L136-L140)
- Disjoint region attribution is a starting point for diagnosis. V19 attribution derives layout regions from evaluated groups and emits source and candidate hashes and cropped pixel evidence. V19 coverage scripts compare the explicitly allowed layer and frame interval. The scope script checks serialized layers and markup within the allowed interval. Its summary mentions 95 native controls, which the script itself does not open or hash. In the V19 status writer, a completed review and the exact GSAP movie hash are required, but there is no assertion for a specific event’s frames or region. A new completion gate should additionally require the exact event-state identity, decoded onset and full criterion-specific native evidence. ## Authoring and editability

The frozen scene has 127 top-level layers and 815 recursive nodes. There are 156 live text nodes, 136 image nodes using 84 distinct asset paths, 287 rectangles, 162 groups, 58 paths and 16 circles. Bubbles, selection shapes, composer, keyboard, waveform and camera geometry can be edited as scene layers. The timed restaurant result, rich message cards and composer are individually constructed. No separate conversation-state file, semantic action event frame, or audio cue map drives this project. An editable iMessage remake can reuse the scene techniques while putting sender, selection, press and send state in a conversation contract. For Director, the useful reference is causal behavior inside Messages, from the dinner prompt through calendar and restaurant selection, then the reply that promises the reminder.
